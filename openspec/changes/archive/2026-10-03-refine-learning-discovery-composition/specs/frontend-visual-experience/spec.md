# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Discovery routes form an original illustrated learning world

Home, catalog, Journey, and Course SHALL present a coherent CodeQuest world through purposeful original illustrations, compact game cues, readable content, and distinct page compositions. Decorative art SHALL support the learning task and SHALL not supply essential text, obscure controls, or imply unavailable product capabilities. The experience SHALL remain usable when an illustration fails to load.

#### Scenario: Visitor moves from entry to Course

- **WHEN** a visitor follows Home through catalog and Journey to a published Course
- **THEN** each page has a distinct, recognizable role within one visual system and the next real learning action stays clear

#### Scenario: Art is unavailable

- **WHEN** a decorative world image fails to load
- **THEN** headings, course identity, available actions, and status explanations remain legible and usable
