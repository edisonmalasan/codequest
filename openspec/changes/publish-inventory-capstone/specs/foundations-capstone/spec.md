## Purpose

Provide the approved Foundations inventory-manager integration project with bounded isolated feedback, required response presence and truthful personal-learning acceptance.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Final capstone extends the instructional journey

The published Foundations journey SHALL retain Q01–Q24 and add one separately counted non-guest capstone with stable identity CAP01, slug inventory-manager, content and assessment version 1.0.0, at the end of Integration after Q24. Its prerequisite SHALL require accepted current-equivalent Q24 completion. Existing instructional snapshots, completion identities and reward history SHALL remain unchanged. Quest, chapter and journey views SHALL derive availability and 25-quest progress from published relationships, without a client completion gate.

#### Scenario: Capstone is available
- **WHEN** an authenticated learner has accepted compatible Q24 completion
- **THEN** backend availability permits the capstone while earlier history remains unchanged

#### Scenario: Guest or locked learner submits
- **WHEN** a guest or authenticated learner without the prerequisite requests capstone acceptance
- **THEN** existing authentication or prerequisite policy denies account completion and rewards

### Requirement: Project assesses taught logic and declared rubric

The capstone SHALL provide explicit record/function contracts, learner-chosen sample records, incomplete helper signatures, graduated hints, an injected defect, a revealing-test explanation and a fresh transfer requirement. Its rubric SHALL cover values/records, decisions, iteration, functions, integration, debug/test reasoning and transfer. Console output SHALL supply accessible textual project presentation. DOM/events, network, packages and asynchronous APIs SHALL NOT be assessed. Functional checks SHALL use varied normal, empty, changed-record and boundary inputs, fit existing limits, accept equivalent alternatives and remain in the isolated Worker. Required explanation and transfer presence SHALL be distinguished from quality review, with no numerical threshold or LLM grading.

#### Scenario: Learner completes project work
- **WHEN** the learner implements the declared functions and runs Check
- **THEN** bounded functional feedback identifies cases while written responses remain required separately and no account completion follows automatically

#### Scenario: Valid alternative or defective code is checked
- **WHEN** reference, equivalent alternative, defective or looping source runs
- **THEN** behavior determines feedback, defective code fails, bounded timeout recovery remains available and no source executes in NestJS

### Requirement: Publication and acceptance claims remain honest

Capstone selection SHALL follow recorded instructional and technical review including actual isolated reference and alternative passes and defective failures. Documentation SHALL identify AI-assisted self-review, preserve provisional F04 values and open learner-observation/F02/F06 beta obligations, and SHALL NOT claim independently verified correctness, reasoning quality, deployment or mastery certification. Accepted completion SHALL use normal owner, version, prerequisite, replay, first-completion XP and backend-acceptance-time streak policy; repeated practice SHALL NOT create repeat rewards.

#### Scenario: Reviewed capstone is selected
- **WHEN** the reviewed manifest and public API are read
- **THEN** only selected capstone material is public, private solutions/review fixtures remain excluded, and passing Check is labeled local and unverified
