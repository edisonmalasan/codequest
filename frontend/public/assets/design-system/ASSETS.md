# CodeQuest design-system assets

All CodeQuest identity SVGs and the Foundations Valley panorama were created specifically for this repository on 2026-09-21. They are project-owned original work and are not derived from third-party game artwork. The panorama was generated from a CodeQuest-specific art direction prompt, then converted lossily to WebP at quality 90 without resizing. The Signal Road, Beacon City, and Daybreak Frontier scenes were generated on 2026-10-03 from original CodeQuest art direction prompts and converted to WebP at quality 82, 80, and 75 respectively without resizing. Research images are not production assets.
